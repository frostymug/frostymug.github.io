What Frost Rocks Is Testing

Frost Rocks is a field notebook with memory: encounters in the world become material for later noticing, reflection, and skill development. Finland proved the capture/review substrate with one real trip. Door County is the next test: can the app support multiple named trips without losing the speed and emotional clarity of the field flow, and can that cleaner structure support repeatable WoZ tutor pilots?

The TestFlight app is evidence, not final product truth. Product decisions should be grounded in field use, exported encounter evidence, and explicit design/architecture review.

Active Workstreams

  • Door County field validation — current priority. Capture bugs, friction, successful moments, screenshots, exports, and workflow observations from real use; separate defects from product insights.
  • WoZ learning pilots — active discovery. Continue testing the evening-debrief/tutor concepts manually against real trip records before automating them.
  • AI tutor / D3 — designed, not built. Preserve the AI Tutor Design Brief, D3 Wedge Spec, three WoZ pilot logs, grounding note, and implementation plan as inputs. Re-sequence implementation behind the multi-trip decision unless Andy explicitly changes priority.
  • Release verification. Verify capture, editing, questions, custom keywords, metadata, photo portability, Review scope reset, map behavior, and both export scopes on-device during the Door County pilot.
