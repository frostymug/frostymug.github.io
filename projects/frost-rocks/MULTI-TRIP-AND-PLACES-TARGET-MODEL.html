Multi-Trip & Places — Target Model

Izzy (Design) | 2026-07-26 | Status: DURABLE TARGET (Andy-approved, 2026-07-26). Not a build spec. The Door County build is the P1 slice of this; see MULTI-TRIP-P1-DOOR-COUNTY-DELTA-SPEC.md. Sources: Andy's 2026-07-26 multi-trip interview (on the flight home from Finland) + the P1 prototype compare page (published-html/multi-trip-v0-explore/) + live repo aspen-frost/frost-rocks.

---

0. Why this note exists

We are adding a second trip (Door County) so we can keep running WoZ tutor pilots. The data and capture scale easily; the hard part is the experience design of living across many trips (~3–15/year, growing). This note captures the durable model we're aiming at, so the small Door County build doesn't paint us into a corner and so the AI-tutor consult and future T1/T3 work inherit a stable frame.

This is the target, not the Door County scope. Door County builds only the P1 slice (§6).

---

1. The load-bearing insight: two verbs, opposite relationships to time

Everything follows from this. There are two different things you do with a trip, and they relate to time in opposite ways:

  • Capture is present-tense and singular. You only ever capture into the current context. "Switching trips" is nonsensical at the shutter — the current context is a fact of where you are in your life, not a selection you make.
  • Review / prep time-travels and is plural. You revisit any past trip (retrospective) and prep any upcoming trip (prospective). Roaming across contexts is the core flow here.

Design consequence: the mechanism for roaming across trips belongs to the review/prep side and must never sit on or near the capture path. This also dissolves the apparent map/timeline inconsistency — the map is a capture-side surface (current context), the timeline is a look-back surface (roams across all).

---

2. The core abstraction: a capture context (Trip or Place)

The real top-level object is not "trip." It's a capture context — the thing an encounter belongs to. There are two flavors, distinguished by time-boundedness:

FlavorBounded?ExampleEnds?Map behavior (long-term)
TripYes — has start/endFinland (Jul 16–25)You mark it overBounded region
PlaceNo — recurringNorth Shore ("My Places")Never; you just visit againAccretes pins across years

"My Places" is Andy's concept: places he returns to repeatedly, accumulating encounters across many visits. A place is not a trip — it's an unbounded, standing context.

This is why the management tab graduates from "Trips" to "Trips & Places."

Data-model implication (target, not Door County): today encounters.trip_id is NOT NULL and everything hangs off getOrCreateDefaultTrip(). The durable target is for an encounter to point at a context (a trip or a place or, transiently, nothing/unfiled) rather than a hard trip_id. We do not build this for Door County — but Door County code must not deepen the getOrCreateDefaultTrip()-everywhere assumption (see §6 discipline).

---

3. Two tabs, two different jobs (this is the P2/P3 hybrid, refined)

The prototype's P2 ("Trips in Review") and P3 ("Trips tab") each felt half-right. The refinement: they are not redundant — they do different jobs.

3.1 The Trips & Places tab = management / lifecycle

The CRUD home for contexts. This is where the set of containers is maintained:

  • Create a trip; mark a trip over; spin up the next trip(s).
  • Create and manage Places (My Places).
  • This replaces today's hard-coding of trips into the app. Right now a trip exists because it's coded in (DEFAULT_TRIP_TITLE). We generalize that into a real flow: end the current trip, create the next.

Not everything about a trip needs to live here — this is lifecycle management, not browsing your memories.

3.2 The Review tab = temporal lenses

Ways to look back at your learning. Trips are one organizing structure inside Review — not the only one, and not managed here (so not every trip needs to appear). Review borrows trips as a scaffold; it does not own them.

The contents are a zoom axis over time:

  • Review Today's Encounters (today) — the D3 evening-debrief door (already in the D3 spec).
  • Review the Trip So Far (this trip) — the T3-ish consolidation door (stubbed today).
  • Review across time (all-time) — working name TBD: "Review Everything" / "Review Across Time" / "General Review". The all-history lens; also the natural home for cross-trip and per-place review (North Shore across years).

Naming flag: "Review" is honest for retrospection but wrong for prep. You cannot review an upcoming trip. So forward-looking prep (T1) for an upcoming trip is reached from the Trips & Places tab, next to the trip being set up — not from Review. This keeps "Review = look back" clean. The exact lens names above deserve their own small IA pass when T1/T3 are designed; don't over-decide now.

3.3 Why this beats plain P2 or plain P3

  • Plain P3 gave two doors that both sort of meant "trips" (a Trips tab and a Review-of-current-trip), which was redundant.
  • Plain P2 put trip management inside Review, but you can't "review" an upcoming trip, and Review shouldn't own the container lifecycle.
  • Hybrid: Trips & Places manages the containers; Review looks through them (with trips as one lens among several, plus places and all-time). No redundancy; each verb has one home.

---

4. The capture rule, amended

Old rule: "Capture always goes to the current trip." New rule: "Capture always goes to the current context, if there is one."

The amendment matters because of Places and the in-between state: at home, between trips, with no active trip, you should still be able to capture. It lands in a Place (e.g., a home place / My Places) or an "unfiled" bucket — never nowhere.

  • The field case is unchanged: on a trip, capture → that trip. Sacred path untouched.
  • We are only defining behavior for the absence of an active trip, instead of assuming one always exists.
  • This is the one change that touches the capture path, so it's handled with care and is out of Door County scope (Door County always has an active trip).

---

5. Trip states

previous · active · upcoming (Andy: "previous," not "closing"). In the target model these carry real behavior:

  • upcoming → surfaces T1 prep (reached via Trips & Places).
  • active → the current capture context; drives the notebook/map/capture.
  • previous → surfaces T3 consolidation and feeds all-time review.

The "in-between" moment is real and important: near a trip boundary you are simultaneously T3-of-the-previous trip and T1-of-the-next (closing Finland while prepping Door County). The model must let a trip wear a state and let both flows run without a hard single-"active"-pointer assumption baked into the UI. (In v1 the states are largely inert labels; the point is not to design them out.)

---

6. What Door County builds (the P1 slice) — pointer

Door County is the first real slice of this model, chosen to be safe under a one-week deadline. It builds:

  • P1 navigation — trip name in the notebook header → quiet dropdown shelf to see other trips (roaming stays off the capture path).
  • A real Trips (& Places-ready) management tab — minimal: create a trip, mark a trip over, set current. This kills the hard-coded-trip approach. (Andy chose to stand this up now rather than retrofit it in September.)
  • Static offline field map, auto-selected per trip (the "floor"). MapLibre/world-map deferred.
  • Capture → current trip (unchanged); timeline scrolls across all trips; map = current trip.

Design discipline for the build (so the target stays reachable):

  • Do not scatter new getOrCreateDefaultTrip() assumptions into new code. Read "the current trip" from one place (a current-context accessor) so the trip→context generalization later is a swap, not a hunt.
  • The Trips tab is built as Trips now but named/structured so "& Places" and the trip/place context split are additive, not a rewrite.
  • Keep the map's per-trip selection behind a seam so MapLibre can replace the static image invisibly.

Full implementation detail: MULTI-TRIP-P1-DOOR-COUNTY-DELTA-SPEC.md.

---

7. Parked / deferred (explicitly not now)

  • My Places (recurring unbounded contexts) — target, not Door County.
  • Capture with no active trip → place/home/unfiled + the trip_id→context data-model generalization.
  • Review temporal-lens IA + naming (today / this-trip / across-time) — needs its own pass with T1/T3.
  • T1 prep and T3 consolidation flows — separate design track (AI tutor).
  • Zoomable all-trips / all-places world map (MapLibre) — the long-term map destination.
  • Trips & Places management depth (editing itineraries, place boundaries, merging) — later.
